### The Theme: Cloud City (Metropolis Architecture)
Cloud City envisions cloud computing infrastructure through the lens of a bustling, high-tech architectural metropolis. Digital systems—like modern cities—rely on resilient foundations: high-density compute skyscrapers, zero-cold-start serverless energy grids, autonomous AI intelligence networks, and hardened perimeter defense.

Organized by the AWS Student Builder Group at PICT, this conference bridges the gap between university theoretical curricula and production-scale industrial engineering. Attendees experience an interactive tech metropolis featuring cinematic shutter gates, vector district maps, single-stage curated masterclasses, live cloud infrastructure teardowns, an afternoon Live Hackathon Arena, and direct connections to tech leaders and recruiters.

## 2. Interactive Experience Districts

Cloud City is divided into four distinct technical districts designed to give builders hands-on exposure to modern cloud stacks:

1. **Compute District (EC2, Containers & Kubernetes):**
   - High-density node provisioning, containerized microservices, and Amazon EKS at scale.
   - Intelligent node autoscaling with Karpenter, eBPF networking via Cilium, and cost optimization.
   - Deep dive into Linux kernel primitives and distributed container runtimes.

2. **Serverless District (AWS Lambda, EventBridge & DynamoDB):**
   - Event-driven reactive architectures, asynchronous choreography, and fan-out patterns.
   - Zero-cold-start optimization using AWS Lambda SnapStart, lightweight runtimes (Rust, Go), and provisioned concurrency.
   - Serverless databases: DynamoDB single-table design, transactions, and global replication.

3. **AI & Vector Grid (Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker & Agents):**
   - Autonomous multi-agent orchestration, state machines, and graph workflows.
   - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using vector databases and dense embeddings.
   - Model evaluation, guardrails, and production LLM cost and latency benchmarks.

4. **SecOps & Governance District:**
   - Zero-trust architecture, automated least-privilege IAM policies, and cloud posture management.
   - Incident detection and automated quarantine using AWS Step Functions and EventBridge.
   - Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform, AWS CDK) security linting and automated policy enforcement.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Who can attend AWS Student Community Day 2027?
**Answer:** Cloud City is open to all students, developers, and cloud enthusiasts! Whether you are a first-year engineering student, final-year graduate, self-taught builder, or hackathon enthusiast from PICT or any college across Pune and India, you are warmly welcomed.

### Q2: Do I need prior AWS or cloud computing experience?
**Answer:** Not at all! Our agenda is specifically architected for student builders. We feature foundational workshops, live step-by-step code walkthroughs, and mentor-assisted sessions so you can deploy your first serverless microservice or AI agent on the spot.

### Q3: What hardware should I bring on summit day?
**Answer:** Bring your laptop, charger, and valid college ID / PRN proof. We provide 1,000 Mbps high-speed Wi-Fi, power connectivity, and sandbox AWS credits.

### Q4: Are food, refreshments, and lunch included in the pass?
**Answer:** Yes, 100%! Every ticket tier includes welcome breakfast with South Indian delicacies, hot filter coffee and Masala Chai breaks, and a lavish full buffet lunch at Dakkhan Banquet Hall.

### Q5: How does the Live Hackathon & Challenge Arena work?
**Answer:** The Live Hackathon takes place in the afternoon inside the Arcade Alley & Hackathon zone within the main hall. You can compete in teams of 2-4 builders to solve realistic cloud crisis and optimization challenges. Top teams take home certificates, bounties, trophies, and AWS cloud credits.

### Q6: When and how will certificates and swag be delivered?
**Answer:** Physical swag kits (heavyweight cotton tee, holographic sticker pack, smart badge) are handed to you in person at the morning check-in counter. Verifiable digital credentials with unique verification IDs are issued via AWS SBG PICT directly to your registered email.
